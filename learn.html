Does pattern trading actually work?

These setups come from small-cap momentum day trading. Knowing them helps you read any chart. Whether they make money is a separate question, and this repo's engine has tested close cousins of them on real Yahoo data with realistic fills. The results are below.

What the numbers teach

  • A high win rate is not an edge. The 85.7% lane lost money once fills were honest. The swing lane wins only 38% of the time and still makes money, because a winner pays about 2R and a loser costs 1R: 0.38 × 2 − 0.62 × 1 = +0.14R per trade before costs.
  • Speed decides intraday setups. A 1-minute trigger that looks clean on a finished chart arrives one to three minutes late on a free data feed. By then the breakout candle has already moved most of the planned reward.
  • The anatomy carries over. Every setup here has the same three parts as the live swing orders: a trigger price above a level, a stop under the structure, and a target set in R. Learn the anatomy here and apply it on slower charts where your execution isn't the weak link.
