Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you join.

What is KamdenAI?+-

KamdenAI.com is a stock signal system built around a focused morning process. It scans the market, narrows the list, posts confirmed signals, and tracks the official result so members can review the system with real outcomes.

What is inside the membership?+-

Members get market-day stock signals, the private community, Signal Results, trade journal tools, classroom content, member profiles, account-based sizing examples, and developer webhooks for sending signal events into other tools.

Does KamdenAI auto-trade for me?+-

No. KamdenAI is a signal system, not an auto-trading broker or trade execution platform. You stay in control of your own account and decide what to do with any signal.

Can I automate around the signals?+-

Yes. KamdenAI includes developer webhooks so members can send signal events into tools like dashboards, alerts, Make, Zapier-style workflows, or their own systems. The webhook gives you the signal data; any automation you build is yours to control.

Are the signals posted every day?+-

KamdenAI is designed for regular market days. When conditions and data are available, members can follow the daily morning sequence: research, get ready, confirmed signals, and results.

What makes the Signal Results section useful?+-

The results ledger shows what happened after confirmed signals, including the official 10:05 outcome. It helps members see the system's recent activity instead of relying on screenshots or claims.

Is this financial advice?+-

No. KamdenAI provides educational analysis, alerts, watchlists, targets, stops, and sizing examples. It does not place trades for you or tell you what you personally should do. You are responsible for your own trading decisions and risk.
