The 7 trades

Read this before believing the 85.7%

  • It is in-sample. These rules were chosen because they won on this month. On 7 trades, the 95% confidence interval around 6 wins runs roughly from 49% to 97% - the true rate could be a coin flip.
  • High win rate was bought with small wins. Taking profit at +0.4R means the average winner makes about $30 while the one loser gave back $55. The edge, if real, is thin: +0.144R per trade.
  • Fewer trades is the mechanism. Most of the improvement came from refusing trades, not from picking better ones.
  • Fill model is optimistic. Executions at the confirmation print, no slippage, no commissions, delayed free data.
  • The real test is forward. The daily 9:05 ET routine keeps running live; its ledger is the out-of-sample record that will confirm or embarrass this page.
